    DIVE MANUAL

    How to play

    1. 1You get five rounds. Every run mixes a Difficulty, a List Demon and three rotating bonus categories.
    2. 2Type a level name and submit. GDBrowser verifies it against the prompt; if unavailable, the app fetches GDHistory live before using a verified offline snapshot. If the exact name still doesn't verify, you'll get a Did you mean? prompt — press Yes to accept the suggested level.
    3. 3The rarer the level — fewer downloads and likes — the higher the round score, up to 100.
    4. 4Levels can repeat across rounds. Your final score is the average of all five rounds.
    QUESTION CATEGORIES

    Difficulty — a level of that difficulty. Demon List — a demon in the current Pointercrate top 150 by list position or rated rank. Gauntlet — one of the five levels in a named Gauntlet. Creator — a rated level by that creator. Trivia — official soundtrack, two-player, featured, epic, star count, length, name shape and rated platformer puzzles.

    RARITY & VALIDITY

    Rarity uses a very soft logarithmic curve with the beta boundaries: 2,000 → 100,000,000 downloads and 1 → 4,000,000 likes, averaged into a 0–100 round score. Popular levels still score respectably.

    Krillion tier names are score bands, not prompt difficulties: 0–10 Plankton, 11–15 Too Clever, 16–30 Schooler, 31–60 Rare, 61–98 Deep Cut, 99–100 One in a Krillion. Show Tier Names is on by default; turn on Show Exact Numbers in Settings to display tier and number together, or switch tier names off for numbers only.

    Only rated, public, non-deleted levels ever count. Creator and gauntlet answers are checked against their exact IDs or fields — no unrated levels, no invented data.
